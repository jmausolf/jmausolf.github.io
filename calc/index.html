Enhanced Financial Retirement Planning Tool

Advanced portfolio growth modeling with dynamic life events

Portfolio Parameters

Planning Years is the full time horizon. Savings and bonus stop after this many years and withdrawals begin.

Retirement Requirements

The rule draws the Required Salary, inflated to the first retirement year and raised with inflation every year after. The other takes the withdrawal rate times that year's starting balance, every year.

Balance Hits (One-time Events)

Add positive amounts for windfalls (inheritance, bonuses) or negative amounts for expenses (tuition, major purchases)

Monthly Savings Hits (Ongoing Changes)

Add positive amounts for income increases or negative amounts for expense increases that reduce savings

Once retirement begins, savings, bonus and earlier monthly changes are set to zero automatically. Hits dated after retirement starts apply as ongoing monthly income (+, e.g. Social Security) or expenses (-).
